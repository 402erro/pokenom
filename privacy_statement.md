PokéNom Discord Bot – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 10 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PokéNom ("the Bot") collects, stores, and processes user data. By adding the Bot to your server or interacting with it, you agree to the data practices described below.
1. Data We Collect

To function properly and maintain your in-game progression, PokéNom processes and stores a limited amount of data. This data is public Discord information or text generated by interacting with the Bot:

    User Identifiers: Unique Discord User IDs, usernames, and profile display names.

    Server Identifiers: Discord Server (Guild) IDs and Channel IDs where the Bot is active.

    Game Progression Data: Your virtual inventory, currency balances, caught items, team configurations, and general gameplay statistics.

    Command Usage: Timestamps and logs of commands sent to the Bot to monitor for errors, performance optimization, and to prevent abuse/exploits.

    Note on Content: PokéNom does not read, log, or store your private messages (DMs) or general chat history. The Bot only processes messages that explicitly invoke its commands.

2. How We Use the Data

The collected data is used strictly for the following purposes:

    To maintain, save, and load your in-game profile and virtual inventory across Discord servers.

    To operate the game's competitive mechanics, global leaderboards, and trading economy.

    To enforce our Terms of Service (such as detecting unauthorized automation or giveaway abuse).

    To debug technical errors, optimize database queries, and improve the Bot's overall performance.

3. Data Storage and Security

    Storage: All data is securely stored in our central database hosted with industry-standard security protocols.

    Retention: Data is stored for as long as you continue to use the Bot. If an account remains completely inactive for an extended period, we reserve the right to prune or archive inactive data to optimize database performance.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We value your privacy. PokéNom never sells, rents, or shares your user data with third-party companies, advertisers, or external services. Data is only visible within the Bot's ecosystem (e.g., displaying your username on a public leaderboard or profile card).
5. User Rights and Data Deletion

You retain full ownership of your data. If you wish to have your gameplay data permanently deleted from our database, you may do so at any time:

    Self-Deletion: Use the designated in-game deletion command if available (e.g., /resetdata or equivalent).

    Manual Request: Join our official Support Server and contact a member of the development team to request a manual wipe of your Discord User ID from our systems.

Please note that data deletion is permanent and irreversible.
6. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our features or platform compliance rules. Any major updates will be communicated through our official support channels or via direct Bot announcements.
